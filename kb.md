# Hotel Knowledge Base

## Check-in and Checkout
- **Check-in:** Check-in time is 3:00 PM, and check-out time is 11:00 AM.
- **Checkout:** Standard check-out is 11:00 AM. Late check-out requests must be confirmed with the front desk.

## Parking and Transportation
- **Parking:** Valet parking is $16 per night. Self-parking is free.

## Pet Policy
- **Pets:** Up to two dogs are allowed per room ($25 per night cleaning fee).

## Cancellation Policy
- **Cancellation:** Cancellations made at least 24 hours prior receive a full refund.

## Wi-Fi and Internet
- **WiFi:** Free high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property. Network details are provided at check-in.

## Breakfast
- **Breakfast:** Complimentary continental breakfast is served daily from 6:00 AM to 10:00 AM in the Main Lobby.

## Amenities and Pool
- **Amenities:** Our fitness center is open 24/7, and the outdoor heated pool is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
- **Pool Hours:** Open daily from 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Located on the 2nd floor terrace.

## House Rules
- **Smoking:** Slumber Inn is a 100% smoke-free property. A $250 cleaning fee applies for smoking in guest rooms.

## Payment and Incidentals
- **Payment Policy:** Major credit cards are required at check-in. Prepaid cards and cash are not accepted for room bookings.
- **Incidentals:** A temporary hold of $50 per night is placed on credit cards for incidental charges, released within 3 to 5 business days post-checkout.

## Accepted Payment Methods
- **Credit Cards:** We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express (AMEX), and Discover. A valid credit card matching the primary guest's photo ID is required at check-in.
- **Digital Wallets:** Apple Pay and Google Pay are accepted for front desk payments.
- **Cash and Prepaid Cards:** Cash and prepaid cards are not accepted for initial room deposits or incidentals.

## Room Amenities
- **In-Room Features:** All rooms feature individual climate control, a mini-refrigerator, coffee maker, iron, ironing board, and hair dryer.
- **Microwaves:** Available upon request or in the 24-hour guest lounge.

## Family and Guest Policies
- **Minimum Age:** The primary guest checking in must be at least 21 years old with a valid photo ID.
- **Cribs and Extra Beds:** Infant cribs are complimentary. Rollaway beds are available for $20 per night in king rooms.

## Luggage and Accessibility
- **Luggage Storage:** Complimentary secure luggage storage is available at the front desk before check-in and after checkout.
- **Accessibility:** ADA-compliant rooms featuring roll-in showers and visual alarms are available upon request.

## Housekeeping and Laundry
- **Housekeeping:** Daily room refresh service is available from 9:00 AM to 3:00 PM. 
- **Laundry Facilities:** Self-service laundry is located on the 3rd floor. Same-day dry cleaning can be arranged through the front desk.

## Dining and Bar
- **On-Site Dining:** The Slumber Lounge is open daily from 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM for dinner and drinks.
- **Room Service:** Available during lounge operating hours with a $5 delivery fee.

## Business and Package Services
- **Business Center:** A complimentary printing and computer station is available 24/7 in the lobby.
- **Package Deliveries:** Guests can ship packages to the hotel ahead of arrival; we hold them securely at the front desk.

## Security and Front Desk
- **Front Desk:** Staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- **In-Room Safes:** Digital programmable safes are installed in all guest rooms at no extra charge.
